What you need to know

CSS — spacing, borders, and one set of colour variables. No design experience: the numbers below are the design.

A marker sees the screen before they see the code. Four rules make it look considered: one accent colour, one spacing step, one corner radius, and no drop shadows except on the thing being dragged. Everything below follows from those.

The node card

150 × 52, radius 10, white, 1 px border. A 5 px coloured stripe down the left says which family it belongs to — trigger, transform, Google, AI, control flow.

Title 13 px semibold; subtitle 11 px muted, and the subtitle is always the setting that matters most: the webhook path, the operation, the sheet name.

State, without a legend

Selected = 2 px accent border. Running = accent border plus a pulsing dot. Failed = red border and a red pill. Never ran = no pill at all.

Item counts sit on the connection, not the node, because that is where the data actually is — and it teaches the item model without a word of explanation.

How to make it look good without a designerPick the spacing from the four values above and never invent a fifth. Give every interactive thing a visible :focus ring in the accent colour. Keep one weight of border. And leave more empty space around the canvas than feels necessary — crowding is what makes student projects look unfinished.
